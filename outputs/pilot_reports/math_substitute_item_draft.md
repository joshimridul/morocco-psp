# Recommended maths item changes

This table is written as a direct recommendation set, not as a piloting plan.

Under the revised ceiling rule, `m21_nv`, `m221`, `m230`, and `m282_nv` are no longer automatic `replace` items.
They remain in this table as optional hardening candidates if the final maths forms still look too easy overall.

| item | old question | new question | reason for change | skill |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `m21_nv` | Read numbers : 6, 0, 3, 9, 2, 8, 1, 4, 7, 5 | Read the numbers: 17, 40, 68, 92. | The current item is at ceiling. Keep numeral reading, but move from isolated digits to two-digit numbers. | Read numerals aloud |
| `m221` | Connect the digital clock and the analog clock with a line: (6:00) | Connect the digital clock and the analog clock with a line: (7:30) | The current item is too easy in grade 2. Keep the clock-matching format, but move from exact hour to half past. | Match digital and analog clocks |
| `m222` | Connect the digital clock and the analog clock with a line: (12:00) | Connect the digital clock and the analog clock with a line: (5:15) | The current item is too easy in grades 2 and 3. Keep the clock-matching format, but move from exact hour to quarter past. | Match digital and analog clocks |
| `m239_nv2` | 12 × 4 | 14 × 3 = | Treated students did much worse than control on the current item. Keep one-digit multiplication, but replace the specific fact. | Multiply by a one-digit number |
| `m240_nv3` | 43 x 2 | 24 × 3 = | Treated students did much worse than control on the current item. Keep two-digit by one-digit multiplication, but use a cleaner fact. | Two-digit by one-digit multiplication |
| `m275` | The students of the third level at the school created a garden in the shape of a rectangle, its length 4 meters long and 3 meters wide. The hill wants to surround it with a fence to protect it the flowers. Calculate the length of the fence that they will need | The students of the third level created a rectangular garden 8 m long and 5 m wide. Calculate the length of fence needed to surround it. | The current item behaved oddly and the wording is clumsy. Keep the perimeter-in-context construct, but simplify and clarify the prompt. | Perimeter of a rectangle in a real-world context |
| `m278_nv3` | What is the title of the story that was read by 1 student only? | Which story was read by the fewest students? | Treated students did worse than control. Keep the graph-reading skill, but make the question wording more direct. | Read a chart or table and identify the least frequent category |
| `m230` | 5 x 6 = | 7 × 8 = | The current item is too easy in grade 4. Keep multiplication-fact fluency, but use a harder fact. | Multiplication fact fluency |
| `m282_nv` | 4x9 = | 15 × 6 = | The current item is too easy in grade 5. Keep one-step multiplication, but raise the difficulty. | One-step multiplication |
| `m294_nv3` | 3/5 + 1/5 = | 2/8 + 3/8 = | Treated students did worse than control. Keep same-denominator fraction addition, but replace the specific item with a cleaner version. | Add fractions with the same denominator |
